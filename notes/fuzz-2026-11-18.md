# Fifth fuzz round, 2026-11-18

Converix NixOS VM, `shell.nix`, the six runtimes of the fourth round
(IronPLC `6e40df2`, matiec `c316fe5`, the OpenPLC fork, STruC++, RuSTy
`e911ee5`, rk), seeds 7001-7400. Every exclusion for a known defect is
on: the base set `literal_args,case_call,mux_expr,not_expr,limit_inverted,ext_minmax`
plus `mux_selector_call,bit_not_compare,call_in_args,call_divisor`, and
per profile `conversions,mux_unsigned` (`wide`), `time_functions,time_mul,time_literal_ms`
(`time`), `string_order,string_insert,string_replace,string_int,ext_concat`
(`strings`), `std_formal` (call profiles) and the structure and array
element exclusions of the earlier rounds.

No new defect:

- IronPLC, `wide` (seeds 7043, 7048, 7075, 7304): each program feeds a
  `SHL` result to `SHR`, `OR` or a comparison without storing it first,
  for example `ob := SHR((vb OR SHL(by0, 3)), 1)`. That is finding 25,
  ironplc#2553 (`ops/shl_width`): the bits shifted beyond the width stay.
  The `shifts` exclusion would hide it; it was left on purpose.
- OpenPLC fork, `strings`: all 15 pass with upstream's `__str_cmp` in a
  patched copy of the fork's `lib`. That is fork item 1 through `=` and
  `<>` (`strings/compare_prefix`).
- rk, `strings`: all 167 are E0301 on `ABS(x) MOD n` as a length or
  position (rk item 3, UDINT lengths).

The 194 undefined `strings` seeds are programs whose every candidate hit an
undefined step: a string over its length or a position out of range, at 10
statements.
